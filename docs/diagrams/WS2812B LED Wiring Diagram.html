LED MATRIX WIRING GUIDE

5× 8×32 WS2812B · RASPBERRY PI PICO W · 5V 10A · Lincoiah LJH074

STEP 01
PSU → Breakout
Plug barrel jack into breakout adapter
Top screw terminal = 5V (+)
Bottom screw terminal = GND (−)
STEP 02
Bus → Each Panel LEFT connector
Red wire: bus 5V → LEFT pin 1 (VCC) — all 5 panels
White wire: bus GND → LEFT pin 2 (GND) — all 5 panels
LEFT pin 3 = DIN (data only, not power)
STEP 03
Bus → Pico W
Red: bus 5V → Pico VSYS pin 39
White: bus GND → Pico GND pin 38
⚠ Unplug USB when powered via VSYS
STEP 04
Data Chain (Pico → P1 → P2 → ... → P5)
Pico GP0 → 330Ω resistor → Panel 1 LEFT DIN
Panel 1 RIGHT DOUT → Panel 2 LEFT DIN
Repeat P2→P3, P3→P4, P4→P5 using pigtail cables
⚠ RIGHT connector VCC and GND are never connected. Power comes from the bus independently per panel, not chained through the right connector. RIGHT only carries DOUT (data out).
STEPS 01–04
Identical to Schema 1
PSU → breakout, bus → LEFT connector, Pico power, data chain all the same
STEP 05 — NEW
Bus → MID connector (all 5 panels)
Red/orange: bus 5V → MID pin 1 (VCC)
White: bus GND → MID pin 2 (GND)
Wire routes below each panel then up into MID — no data ever
⚠ MID connector = power only. 2 pins: VCC and GND. No data pin exists. Routing: wire drops from the bus, travels below the panel in the gap, then rises into the MID connector from below. Do this for all 5 panels.
💡 Why injection matters: Without it, power travels the full width of 256 LEDs from the left edge only. PCB trace resistance causes a voltage drop — LEDs on the right appear dimmer. Injecting at the center halves the trace distance and equalizes brightness across the whole panel.
